# Follow-up matrix: [event or push]

`<root>/events/<event>/follow-up-matrix.md`. The source list (badge scans, exports, notes) is saved alongside as `source-list.md`; rebuild the matrix from it when it changes. One row per promise; a person with two promises gets two rows.

| Person | Org | Promised | Material owed | Due | Status |
|--------|-----|----------|---------------|-----|--------|
| [name] | [org] | [what was promised] | [which asset] | [date, or blank + "needs date"] | [open / sent / done] |

open = nothing sent. sent = material left the building (the owner sent it). done = the recipient responded, or the owner closes the loop.

The matrix is the record for event promises; the weekly carry-forward is the record for everything else. An item lives in one, not both.
